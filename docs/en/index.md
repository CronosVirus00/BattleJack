# What is Battlejack?
Battlejack is 1v1 card game played with standard decks of cards. Players fight on 3 lanes by placing cards, activating their effects and bluffing: the aim is to get closer to the lane score without busting.
The game is inspired by TCG like Magic and Onepiece, with their strategic dynamics, combined with the blackjack mechanics. Each suits has different [powers](./Potere%20dei%20Semi.md) to choose from, allowing the players to play they way it suits (pun intended) them best.

## What do you need to start to play?
A standard deck of cards (52 cards) per player; remove the court cards (K, Q, J) and jokers if they are present. 
If you have only one deck of card; check out the [drafting](link) of the game.

A way to keep track of the score and of the turns of play. You can use the [game scoreboard]((./assets/segnapunti.png)) or copy it on paper. Some powers might require the use of tokens.

The [rule book](./Regolamento.md), obviously.
